Why Visit Donegal Town?

Rich Heritage

Discover centuries of Irish history at Donegal Castle and the ancient Diamond Square, where stories of chieftains and traders echo through time.

Stunning Landscapes

Surrounded by dramatic mountains, pristine beaches, and the beautiful Donegal Bay - nature lovers will find paradise here.

Warm Hospitality

Experience genuine Irish welcome in cozy pubs, friendly restaurants, and charming B&Bs that make you feel right at home.

Featured Attractions

Donegal Castle

A magnificent 15th-century fortress standing proudly by the River Eske, offering a glimpse into Ireland's medieval past.

Diamond Square

The historic heart of Donegal Town, surrounded by colorful buildings and buzzing with local life and traditional music.

Donegal Bay

Picturesque waterfront perfect for scenic walks, boat tours, and enjoying fresh seafood with breathtaking views.
