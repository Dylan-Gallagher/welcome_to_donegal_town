Attractions

Discover the treasures of Donegal Town

🏰 Donegal Castle

Standing proudly by the River Eske, Donegal Castle is a magnificent 15th-century fortress that tells the story of Ireland's chieftains. Built by the O'Donnell family, this tower house and later Jacobean manor has witnessed centuries of history. Explore its beautifully restored rooms, walk the ancient stone walls, and learn about the legendary Red Hugh O'Donnell. The castle offers stunning views of the river and surrounding countryside.

Opening Hours: Daily 10am - 6pm (April - September), 10am - 4pm (October - March)

Admission: Adults €8, Students/Seniors €6, Children €4

💎 Diamond Square

The historic heart of Donegal Town, Diamond Square is where the town's story began. Surrounded by colorful traditional buildings and charming shops, this bustling square is the perfect place to soak in local atmosphere. The famous Market Cross monument stands at its center, commemorating the town's rich trading history. Enjoy street performers, local art displays, or simply sit and watch the world go by with a coffee from one of the nearby cafes.

Best Time to Visit: Summer months when farmers' markets and events take place

Features: Historic monuments, local shops, cafes, street entertainment

⚓ The Quay Area

Donegal Town's picturesque waterfront offers stunning views of Donegal Bay and the Bluestack Mountains. Stroll along the promenade, watch fishing boats come and go, or enjoy fresh seafood at waterfront restaurants. The Quay is also the departure point for boat tours to the nearby islands and scenic bay cruises. At sunset, the area transforms into a magical spot with golden light reflecting off the water.

Activities: Walking, boat tours, seafood dining, photography

Highlights: Bay cruises, island tours, scenic views, fishing heritage

🏛️ The Franciscan Friary

A hidden gem nestled in the town, the Franciscan Friary dates back to the 15th century. These atmospheric ruins offer a peaceful escape and a glimpse into monastic life in medieval Ireland. The friary's stone arches and peaceful surroundings make it a favorite spot for contemplation and photography. Information boards explain the friary's fascinating history and its role in Irish religious heritage.

Location: Near Diamond Square, town center

Access: Free entry, open during daylight hours

🌊 Donegal Bay

The crown jewel of Donegal's natural attractions, Donegal Bay offers breathtaking scenery at every turn. Framed by the majestic Bluestack Mountains and dotted with small islands, the bay is a paradise for nature lovers and photographers. Take a scenic drive along the coast, enjoy water sports, or simply relax on one of the beautiful beaches. The bay is also home to diverse wildlife including seals, dolphins, and numerous bird species.

Best Views: From the Quay, Killybegs Harbor, or nearby headlands

Activities: Scenic drives, wildlife watching, water sports, photography

🎭 Donegal Craft Village

Discover the artistic soul of Donegal at the Craft Village, where local artisans showcase their traditional skills. Watch skilled craftsmen and women at work creating pottery, textiles, jewelry, and more. The village features working studios where you can purchase unique, handcrafted souvenirs and gifts. Each piece tells a story of Irish tradition and modern creativity. Regular workshops and demonstrations offer visitors a chance to learn these ancient crafts.

Opening Hours: Daily 10am - 6pm (seasonal)

Specialties: Irish tweed, pottery, jewelry, textiles, traditional crafts
